Contoso Distribution Center – Safety Procedures

Fargo Distribution Center

1. Introduction

This safety manual outlines procedures for employees working at the Contoso Fargo Regional Distribution Center during the facility expansion project. It reflects temporary changes to traffic flow, equipment locations, emergency routes, and construction zone protocols.

2. General Safety Rules

  • Wear all required personal protective equipment (PPE) at all times: safety vest, hard hat, steel toe boots, gloves, and eye protection.
  • Report unsafe conditions immediately to your supervisor.
  • Follow posted signs and warnings throughout the facility.
  • Do not enter restricted construction zones unless authorized.

3. Construction Zone Protocols

  • Construction areas are restricted; only authorized personnel may enter.
  • Temporary barriers and signage will indicate hazardous areas.
  • Noise levels may be elevated; hearing protection is required in marked areas.
  • Be alert for heavy equipment including cranes, lifts, and concrete trucks.
  • Walk only in designated pedestrian lanes near construction zones.

4. Updated Warehouse Traffic Flow

  • New temporary pedestrian walkways have been created to route foot traffic away from construction.
  • Forklift lanes have been rerouted — follow updated floor markings.
  • Speed limits for forklifts reduced to 3 mph near construction boundaries.
  • Loading dock access may rotate based on project phase; check daily notices.

5. Updated PPE Requirements

  • Hard hats are now required in Zones A, B, and C.
  • High-visibility outerwear must be worn in all staging areas.
  • Respirators required when working near insulation or cutting operations.
  • Additional protective eyewear must be worn near welding activities.

6. Emergency Procedures During Expansion

  • Several emergency exits have been temporarily relocated. Updated exit maps are posted at every hallway junction and team station.
  • In case of evacuation, follow the new muster point assignments listed below:
  • West Wing Staff — Parking Lot C (near shipping entrance)
  • East Wing Staff — Green Space south of main gate
  • Fire extinguishers and first-aid kits have been repositioned to remain accessible during construction.
  • Emergency communication systems will announce any route changes immediately.

7. Material Handling and New Equipment Safety

  • The expansion introduces new pallet rack systems — only certified staff may operate them.
  • Additional forklifts have been deployed; operators must complete refresher training.
  • Do not store materials in temporary passageways or construction staging zones.

8. Hazard Communication Updates

  • New chemicals used in construction (sealants, paints, solvents) are documented in the updated SDS binder located in the Safety Office.
  • All workers must review SDS sheets before working near new materials.
  • Ventilation systems may be temporarily rerouted — report odors or fumes immediately.

9. Incident Reporting

  • All injuries, near-misses, or equipment issues must be reported using the updated digital incident form.
  • Supervisors must document construction-related hazards and notify the project liaison.
