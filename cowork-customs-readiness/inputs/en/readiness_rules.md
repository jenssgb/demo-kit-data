# Document readiness SOP

Fictional internal Contoso SOP, not a statement of customs law. Scenario clock: 2026-10-14 09:00 Europe/Berlin.

Compare shipment, seller/buyer, goods description, quantity, cartons, net/gross kg between invoice, packing and booking. Internal readiness requires a supplied origin statement and a consistent current document set. A supplier statement is evidence for review, not verified legal origin. Use current packing v1 initially. A corrected v2 may supersede it only when explicitly supplied by the user. Preserve version history. Register states NEW / BLOCKED / READY_FOR_REVIEW. Commercial invoice stays unmodified. Tariff classification, regulatory checks and formal origin review are for the customs specialist; do not infer legal clearance. Queries go to Teresa Sac.
